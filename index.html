Eco Friendly, Low Waste Art

Creating art that brings joy and has a minimal impact on the planet is at the heart of Knots and Embers' ethos.

I make every effort to souce all my materials responsibly. All the wood used is locally-sourced from felled sick or dangerous trees. The oil used for sealing the pieces is natural and vegan. Any paint used is ethically-sourced and made from natural pigments.

Meet The Artist

I'm Hannah and I'm the face behind Knots and Embers. I'm a self-taught pyrography artist living in the the Buckinghamshire countryside in the UK.

I love all things nature and yoga, which is what inspires a lot of my work, but if you have an idea for a theme for a custom commission, I'd love to hear about it and find a way to bring it to life.
